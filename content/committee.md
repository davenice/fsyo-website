# Committee

As a charity FSYO relies on volunteers and has roles open to all supporters.

We currently have 10 trustees made up of the ensemble musical directors and members whose responsibility it is to ensure the aims of the charity are being met. In addition we rely on volunteers to help with concerts and fundraising. There are several key roles for which we welcome offers of support from friends and the parents of members of the ensembles to carry out.

The Friends committee meet every few months.
