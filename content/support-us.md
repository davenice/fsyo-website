FSYO needs to raise money to support the Youth Orchestras of Southampton. There are many ways you can contribute.

## FSYO Membership

Family membership costs just £10 a year. FSYO membership is optional but very much appreciated, and subscriptions form an important part of our income. You do not have to have children in our ensembles to be a member — many families continue their membership after their children have grown up.

Please complete a [membership form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScbeffUw_kyhXg_xUYin0rwBhGnRQaWJyJoUTy4rWWK6zzYrw/viewform?embedded=true) to join.

## Supporting us at concerts

On concert day the Friends pay the costs for hiring the venue, any necessary equipment, and for printing programmes and tickets. You can support us by:

* Buying tickets and programmes
* Donating raffle prizes and buying raffle tickets
* Advertising our concerts and encouraging others to attend

## Where does the money go?

All donations go directly into supporting the young people in our orchestras. None of the volunteers on the committee or at events are paid — we are passionate about the youth orchestras and happy to contribute our time for free.

The Friends maintain a pot for each ensemble from money raised at their concerts. An orchestra can draw on their own pot for example to fund specialist sectional coaching, music and instruments, workshops, residential courses, or concert venue hire.

Income from membership subscriptions and the Pyramid Concert goes into a general fund available to any ensemble, from the junior string groups up to SYO.

*As a registered charity we are bound by Charity Commission rules and our finances must always be transparent.*
