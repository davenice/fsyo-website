[![](/_oldsite2/images/smh.jpg)](http://southamptonmusichub.org/ "opens a new window")[**Southampton Music Hub**](http://southamptonmusichub.org/ "Opens a new window")

The Southampton Music Hub is a strong network of organisations working collaboratively to maximise musical and artistic opportunity for young people. The Hub website provides information on music lessons, individual and group instrumental tuition, musical activity, and concerts in Southampton. The Hub Partnership is a vibrant network of local, regional and national organisations that share a common goal – to offer an ambitious range of musical activity and high quality music making to challenge and engage all young people.

[**Southampton Music Services**](http://www.southampton.gov.uk/schools-learning/in-school/music-services/default.aspx "Opens a new window") (SMS)

SMS brings musical resource, specialism and activity to all communities in our city. They provide ensembles covering a wide range of musical styles and disciplines.

[Visit the SMS Ensembles page](http://www.southampton.gov.uk/schools-learning/in-school/music-services/ensembles.aspx "Opens a new window") listing the impressive musical range.

[View the Ensemble structure](http://www.southampton.gov.uk/Images/Ensemble-Structure-2015-16_tcm63-365631.pdf "Opens a new window") that provides a progression route for every young musician throughout the city.

In addition to delivering specialist music curriculum teaching in schools, SMS also provides weekly instrumental and vocal tuition, concerts, special events, family music making, workshops and holiday courses.

Copyright free music

[**MUSOPEN**](https://musopen.org/ "opens a new window")  
Musopen is a 501(c)(3) non-profit focused on increasing access to music by creating free resources and educational materials. We provide recordings, sheet music, and textbooks to the public for free, without copyright restrictions. Put simply, our mission is to set music free.
