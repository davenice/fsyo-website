The Friends of Southampton Youth Orchestras (FSYO) is a charitable organisation whose aim is to support the various orchestral ensembles that are part of the Southampton Youth Orchestras. We have been supporting the orchestras since 1971. FSYO is a Registered Charity, no. 283789.

We support the [ensembles](/ensembles/) in many ways, for example by providing new instruments and music, and by giving financial support for tours, residential weekends and specialist sectional support. This is in addition to the funding from the Southampton Music Services which pays for the orchestra conductors and rehearsal venues.

**You can support us by becoming a Member**

Membership costs just £10 per year per family, or £40 for four years. All this money goes to supporting the orchestras. [Click here to become a member](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScbeffUw_kyhXg_xUYin0rwBhGnRQaWJyJoUTy4rWWK6zzYrw/viewform?embedded=true)

Subscriptions are an important part of our income, and we welcome as many new members as possible each year. For Acorn, Elgar, and SYS concerts, we make a retiring collection towards FSYO. For SYCS and SYO concerts we run a raffle and sell programmes. We always need additional help to organise these, and rely on parents to provide raffle prizes too.

There are practical ways to help as well. For some concerts, help is needed loading and unloading vans. Some venues need ushers, for instance Thornden Hall. These are coordinated through FSYO.

## Committee

As a charity FSYO relies on volunteers. We currently have 10 trustees made up of the ensemble musical directors and members whose responsibility it is to ensure the aims of the charity are being met. The Friends committee meets every few months.
