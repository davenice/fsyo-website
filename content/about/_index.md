The Friends of Southampton Youth Orchestras (FSYO) is a charitable organisation whose aim is to support the various orchestral ensembles that are part of the Southampton Youth Orchestras. We have been supporting the orchestras since 1971.

**The Pyramid**

The ensembles form a pyramid of young players starting with a broad base of those gaining their first experience of orchestral playing, from Acorn strings, through to [Elgar Strings](/wp/ensembles/#elgar), [Southampton Youth Strings (SYS)](/wp/ensembles/#sys), [Southampton Youth Concert Sinfonia (SYCS)](/wp/ensembles/#sycs) and all the way up to the [Southampton Youth Orchestra (SYO)](/wp/ensembles/#syo) itself, one of the UK’s foremost youth orchestras.

Most of the [FSYO committee members](/committee/) are parents of current players. Musical directors are also represented *ex officio*.

We support the orchestras in many ways, for example by providing new instruments and music, and by giving financial support for tours, residential weekends and specialist sectional support. This is in addition to the funding from the Southampton Music Services which pays for the orchestra conductors and rehearsal venues.

**You can support us by becoming a Member**

Membership is costs just £10 per year per family, or £40 for four years. All this money goes to supporting the orchestras. [Click here to become a member](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScbeffUw_kyhXg_xUYin0rwBhGnRQaWJyJoUTy4rWWK6zzYrw/viewform?embedded=true)

Subscriptions are an important part of our income, and we welcome as many new members as possible each year. There are many other ways to support your child, and others, in making music.

For Acorn, Elgar, and SYS concerts, we make a retiring collection towards FSYO. For SYCS and SYO concerts we run a raffle and sell programmes. We always need additional help to organise these. We rely on parents to provide raffle prizes, too!

There are practical ways to help, too. For some concerts, help is needed loading and unloading vans – SIOW music will often ask for volunteers for this. Some venues need ushers, for instance Thornden Hall. These are coordinated through FSYO.

FSYO is a Registered Charity, no. 283789.
