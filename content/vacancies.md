### Want to help out the FSYO?

The FSYO depends on the contribution of time and commitment of voluntary helpers. Committee members give up their time to make sure that the young people in the orchestras are able to benefit from the wide range of support that the FSYO offers.

There are several roles that are vital to the running of FSYO. Please take this opportunity to make a huge difference by supporting us.

We need people to take on the following roles:

* **Membership Secretary**
* **Social Media Administrator**  
  *If you would like to take up either of these vital and rewarding positions, please don’t hesitate to get in touch with the Committee as soon as possible.*

  [Apply now](mailto:info@fsyo.org.uk?subject=Vacancy%20query)

Please [contact us](/contact-us/) if you’d like to help out in a role or in any other way. Your help can make a real difference.
