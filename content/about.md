The Friends of Southampton Youth Orchestras (FSYO) is a charitable organisation that has supported Southampton's youth orchestras since 1971. We provide new instruments and music, financial support for tours, residential weekends and specialist coaching — supplementing the funding from Southampton Music Services that pays for conductors and rehearsal venues. FSYO is a Registered Charity, no. 283789.

We rely entirely on volunteers and the generosity of our supporters. Membership costs just £10 per year per family, and every pound goes directly to the orchestras. Find out more on our [Support Us](/support-us/) page.
